We examine whether large language models can interpret social signals and behavioral patterns in real patient–provider conversations, and what that means for automated analysis of clinical communication. Accepted at the Conference on Health, Inference, and Learning (CHIL) 2026.

The preprint is posted on arXiv as “SocialLM: Social Signal Processing of Patient-Provider Communication using LLMs and Contextual Aggregation.”
