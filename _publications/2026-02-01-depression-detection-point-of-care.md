This work analyzes linguistic signals from routine primary-care encounters to detect depression at the point of care, without requiring dedicated screening interviews. We characterize which features of patient and clinician language are most informative and discuss implications for ambient clinical AI. Accepted as a poster at the AMIA 2026 Annual Symposium.
