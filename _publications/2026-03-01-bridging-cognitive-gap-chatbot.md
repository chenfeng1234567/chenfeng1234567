We co-designed and evaluated a voice-enabled community chatbot with older adults, focusing on cognitive accessibility. The paper details our participatory design process, the resulting interaction patterns, and a field evaluation in a retirement-community setting. Accepted as a full paper at the AMIA 2026 Annual Symposium.
