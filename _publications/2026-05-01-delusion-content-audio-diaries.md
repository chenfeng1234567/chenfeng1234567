We designed a four-level prompt-engineering framework to extract delusional themes and affective and behavioral responses from naturalistic speech transcripts. A multi-agent LLM pipeline (GPT-OSS, Qwen, and GLM) performs fine-grained multi-label annotation, and we benchmarked its consistency against human annotations. Accepted at the ACL 2026 Workshop on Computational Linguistics and Clinical Psychology (CLPsych).
