We study how communication-style variation — directness, formality, verbosity, politeness, emotional tone, and grammaticality — affects enterprise email, retrieval, and tool-use agents. The work introduces a style taxonomy and an automated framework that generates controlled linguistic variants while preserving semantic intent, then evaluates task completion, retrieval quality, and tool-use reliability, including multilingual and dialectal variation. Accepted at the Workshop on Evaluation of Interactive Agents (IAEval) @ NeurIPS 2026; an extended version has been submitted to NAACL.

The preprint is posted on arXiv under the title “Lost in the Request: How Communication Variation Disrupts Retrieval and Action in Email Agents.”
